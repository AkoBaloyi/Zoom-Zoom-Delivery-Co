WHY THESE ARE HERE AT ALL
The tyre smoke was rendering as plain squares because the particle material
had no texture, so every particle was an untextured quad. A soft-edged
grayscale puff is the entire fix, and there is no reason to draw one by hand
when a public domain pack of them exists.

The tyre marks use FX_SkidStreak sampled as a horizontal band rather than
the whole image, which is what gives a mark soft edges across the tyre width
while tiling seamlessly along its length. See markTextureUInset and
markTextureV on the SkidMarks component.

The rest of the pack, 99 sprites including dirt, flame, scorch, trace, light
and muzzle variants, is at the URL above under the same licence if more are
needed.
